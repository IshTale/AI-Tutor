# **1. The Visual Layout**

Since the AI is driving the interaction, the UI should be clean and focus heavily on the
whiteboard, removing the clutter of traditional drawing apps.


●​ **The Main Stage (Viewport):** An infinite, edge-to-edge container holding the
whiteboard. This is where the panning and zooming happen.
●​ **The AI Status Panel (Floating):** A minimalistic overlay, perhaps in the corner, showing:
○​ An audio visualizer (so the student knows the AI is speaking/listening).
○​ A live transcript of the conversation.
○​ An "Agent Activity" indicator (e.g., "OpenClaw is fetching a diagram..." or
"Generating new visual...").
●​ **Student Controls:** A simple floating action button for "Push to Talk" or "Interrupt,"
allowing the student to ask a question mid-explanation.
**●​** **File Upload:** Students can upload a syllabus, HW assignments, and a textbook for the
agent to explain or expand upon. They should also be able to select a specific file that
they want to focus on. They should only be shown files that the uploaded on the
interface.

# **2. The Rendering Architecture (The Layering System)**


To keep performance high and decouple the static images from the dynamic animations, you
should stack multiple transparent layers on top of each other inside a master container.


|Layer (Botot m to Top)|Technology|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|**1. The Wrapper**|HTML <div>|Acts as the camera. You<br>apply**CSS Transforms** <br>(transform: scale(2.5)<br>translate(x, y);) to this div to<br>handle the zooms and pans.<br>The browser's GPU handles<br>this natively, meaning zero<br>JavaScript overhead for<br>camera moves.|
|**2. The Image Layer**|HTML <canvas> or <img>|Displays the static<br>whiteboard states<br>generated by Gemini 3<br>Flash Image. When a new<br>image arrives, it crossfades<br>over the old one.|


|3. The Ink Layer|HTML5 <canvas>|If OpenClaw or another<br>agent pulls in external<br>assets (like a PNG of a<br>map), they are drawn onto<br>this layer so they sit on top<br>of the base whiteboard.|
|---|---|---|
|**4. The Pointer Layer**|HTML5 <canvas>|The top-most transparent<br>layer. This is cleared and<br>redrawn 60 times a second<br>using<br>requestAnimationFrame to<br>render the Bézier curve<br>laser pointer and its fading<br>trail.|
